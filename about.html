Our Story

Arch Electric was founded in 2009 with a simple mission: provide honest, high-quality electrical work to our Central Coast neighbors. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a trusted name across Monterey, Santa Cruz, and San Benito counties.

As a family-owned business, we treat every customer's home like our own. We believe in doing the job right the first time, being upfront about pricing, and standing behind our work.

Over 15 years later, we're still driven by the same values that started it all — quality craftsmanship, honest communication, and a deep commitment to the communities we serve.
