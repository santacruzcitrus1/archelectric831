Our Story

Arch Electric began as a family business with over 20 years of electrical experience serving the Central Coast. What started with deep roots in the electrical trade has grown into a trusted name across Santa Cruz, Monterey, San Benito, and Santa Clara counties.

As a family-owned business, we treat every customer's property like our own. We believe in doing the job right the first time, being upfront about pricing, and standing behind our work. A+ is my only standard — that means no shortcuts, quality materials, and craftsmanship you can depend on.

With our C-10 contractor license #945992 and full insurance coverage, we handle 15-30 jobs per month ranging from $2K-5K each. No job too big or too small. We do them all.
