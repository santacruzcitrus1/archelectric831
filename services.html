Our Services

Quality electrical work for every need — residential, commercial, and agricultural.

🔌

Electrical Panel Upgrades

Your electrical panel is the heart of your home's power system. If your panel is outdated, overloaded, or a known fire hazard brand, it's time for an upgrade.

  • 100 to 200 amp panel upgrades
  • Replacement of dangerous panels (Zinsco, Federal Pacific, Challenger)
  • Sub-panel installation
  • Main breaker upgrades
  • Meter base replacement
🏠

Residential Electrical

From new construction to remodels, we provide complete residential electrical services for your home.

  • New circuit installation
  • Outlet and switch installation/replacement
  • Ceiling fan installation
  • Whole-house rewiring
  • GFCI and AFCI protection
  • Smoke and CO detector installation
  • EV charger installation
🌾

Agricultural Electrical

The Central Coast's agricultural industry has unique electrical needs. We specialize in farm and ranch electrical work.

  • Barn and outbuilding wiring
  • Irrigation pump electrical
  • Equipment power connections
  • Agricultural lighting
  • 3-phase power installation

Troubleshooting & Repair

Electrical problems can be frustrating and dangerous. We diagnose and fix issues quickly and safely.

  • Circuit tracing and diagnostics
  • Flickering light repair
  • Tripped breaker investigation
  • Ground fault troubleshooting
  • Hot outlet/switch repair
  • Code violation corrections

Lighting

  • Security and motion-sensor lighting
  • LED retrofit and upgrades
  • Dimmer switch installation

New Construction & Remodels
