Our Services

No job too big or too small. We do them all.

🔌

Panel Upgrades & Changes

Upgrading outdated electrical panels is essential for safety and modern electrical demands. We replace dangerous panels including Federal Pacific, Zinsco, and outdated fuse boxes with modern circuit breaker panels.

🚗

EV Charger Installation

Ready to go electric? We install Level 2 EV charging stations for Tesla, Chevy Bolt, Nissan Leaf, and all electric vehicles. From basic 240V outlets to smart charging systems.

🔋

Solar & Battery Backup

Maximize your solar investment with battery backup systems. We install and wire Tesla Powerwall, Enphase Encharge, and other battery storage systems to keep your lights on.

⚡

Generator Backup Systems

Never lose power again. We install and wire standby generators including Generac, Kohler, and other backup power systems. Automatic transfer switches ensure seamless power.

🏠

Full House Rewiring

Older homes need modern wiring. We specialize in complete electrical overhauls — replacing knob & tube wiring, upgrading to modern code, ensuring safety and reliability.

🔧

New Construction & Commercial

From residential new builds to commercial projects, we handle electrical rough-in, finish work, and everything in between. Licensed for both residential and commercial work.
